Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know about design?

No. You pick a style and how much you want to change, and the AI suggests the rest. You can switch each change on or off.

Are the photos real?

They are images generated with artificial intelligence from your photos and are labelled as such. They are a guide to help you decide, not a technical project.

What happens to the photos of my home?

Your projects are stored on your phone. To create each proposal, the photo is sent to Google’s AI (Gemini) on its paid tier, which does not use your data for training.

Are product prices reliable?

They are each shop’s reference prices. Some shopping links are affiliate links: if you buy through them we receive a small commission at no cost to you.

Which languages is it in?

The website and the app are in Spanish, Basque, English, Catalan and French. In the app you change it in Settings.
